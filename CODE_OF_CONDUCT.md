# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in Caliper a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people new to 3D, CAD or code
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected, and
  learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address or email, without
  their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Responsibilities and enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards,
and will take fair corrective action in response to behaviour they find
inappropriate. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that do not align with this code of conduct.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the
repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, marking it as a conduct
report, or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

Consequences, depending on severity: a private correction, a warning, a
temporary ban from community spaces, or a permanent ban.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all community spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
